01Why this project exists

NAZZTEC's current outreach positions the company as a global technology consultancy spanning cybersecurity, cloud, managed services, digital transformation, and staff augmentation — delivered from India, Saudi Arabia, and now the United States. The existing web presence doesn't reflect that scale or positioning.

This brief covers the first UAT pass: one fully designed homepage plus the core page set, built to read as an enterprise consulting firm — in the register of Accenture, Deloitte, or Cognizant — rather than a generic IT services vendor.

02Scope of this UAT

Deliberately narrow: one polished homepage and a handful of core pages, not a full 15-page site. The goal is to validate direction before scaling out content.

Built for this round

  • Homepage — all 12 sections
  • Services index + one reusable service-detail template
  • Staff Augmentation section
  • Webflow build spec (classes, CMS schema)

Deferred / needs input

  • About Us, Careers, Contact — content pending
  • Insights — CMS scaffolded, no articles yet
  • Industries — placeholder only, needs client confirmation
  • Real logo, brand assets, verified contact details

03Design direction

The brief was explicit: avoid a generic startup/IT-vendor look. The direction pulls from enterprise consulting and audit conventions rather than SaaS patterns — sharp corners over rounded cards, hairline borders over drop shadows, a capability "index" instead of a scrolling icon grid.

Brand Blue Ink / dark sections Signal accent Slate / secondary text

Typography: Fraunces (serif) for headlines to read premium rather than templated, Public Sans for body copy, and IBM Plex Mono for reference codes and data — location tags, framework labels, and the eight service-line codes (CRC, TAA, PRC, BCR, CID, MSV, DTA, PCS), styled the way an audit workpaper index would reference them.

04Homepage structure

01Navigation — Home, Services, Staffing, Industries, About, Contact
02Hero — "Technology. Talent. Transformation." + India / Saudi Arabia / United States coverage ledger
03Global presence — three location cards (India, Saudi Arabia, United States)
04Capability index — 8 service lines as a reference-coded list, not a card grid
05Staff Augmentation — talent domains + "Request Talent" CTA
06Why NAZZTEC — global delivery, expertise, flexible engagement, end-to-end capability
07Framework & regulatory expertise — ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, GDPR, PDPL
08Digital transformation — AI, ML, analytics, Power BI, automation (dark section)
09Engagement models — consulting, project delivery, managed services, staff augmentation
10Industries — placeholder, pending client confirmation
11Closing CTA — "Let's solve your next technology challenge"
12Footer — services, company, locations, social
